## 1. Project Overview

This task focuses on researching the applications of the Internet of Things (IoT) in smart agriculture.

The research explores how IoT technologies, sensors, wireless communication and intelligent monitoring systems help improve agricultural productivity and resource management.

## 2. Objectives

- Understand the role of IoT in modern agriculture.
- Study IoT architecture and its working principles.
- Explore sensor-based agricultural monitoring.
- Understand smart irrigation and automated farming systems.
- Identify the benefits and challenges of IoT implementation in agriculture.

## 3. Topics Covered

- Introduction to Smart Agriculture
- IoT Architecture and Working Principles
- Major Components and Technologies
- Real-World Applications of IoT in Agriculture
- Benefits and Advantages
- Challenges and Limitations
- Future Scope of IoT in Agriculture
- Conclusion and References

## 4. Technologies Discussed

- Soil Moisture Sensors
- Wireless Sensor Networks
- Wi-Fi and Zigbee
- LoRa and LoRaWAN
- MQTT Communication Protocol
- Cloud Computing
- Edge Computing
- Artificial Intelligence

## 5. Learning Outcomes
